### 1. Introduction
SUKOON ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free, privacy-first, offline Quran companion designed to help you read, reflect, and learn the Holy Quran peacefully. We firmly believe your spiritual journey, reading habits, and personal reflections belong strictly to you. 

**SUKOON does not require an account, does not serve advertisements, does not track you, and does not sell or transmit your personal data to remote servers.**

---

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
* **No Account or Personal Identity:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or social media logins.
* **No Cloud Reflection Syncing:** Your personal reflections, bookmarks, and reading milestones are stored exclusively on your physical device using Android's local Room database and DataStore.
* **No Advertising Identifiers:** The app contains zero advertising SDKs (no AdMob, no Meta Audience Network, no Unity Ads, etc.).
* **No Behavioral Analytics:** The app does not integrate Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or third-party usage trackers.

---

### 3. Permissions & Device Data Usage

#### A. Location (Coarse & Fine)
* **Purpose:** If you choose to tap **"Detect GPS"** on the Prayer Times screen, your device's native location is queried once to calculate accurate sunrise and prayer times (**Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha**) for your regional coordinates.
* **On-Device Calculation:** All astronomical prayer calculations run completely on your phone using our bundled offline mathematical engine.
* **No Continuous Tracking:** SUKOON never tracks your location in the background.
* **Manual Alternative:** Location permission is entirely optional. You can manually select your city from the bundled city list without granting location permissions.

#### B. Notifications
* **Purpose:** Used strictly to deliver scheduled local prayer reminders and optional daily reflection notifications at the times you configure.
* **Offline Scheduling:** Notifications are scheduled locally via Android's `AlarmManager` without remote push notification servers.

---

### 4. Data Storage and Deletion
All app data (reading history, bookmarks, reflections, and settings) resides strictly in your device's private app sandbox. You can permanently erase all stored data at any time by:
1. Navigating to **Settings $\to$ Reset Progress / Delete Reflections** in the app.
2. Or clearing the app's storage via **Android Settings $\to$ Apps $\to$ SUKOON $\to$ Clear Data**.
3. Uninstalling the application immediately removes all locally cached databases and preferences.
