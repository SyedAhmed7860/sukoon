Support & Help Center

Application: SUKOON (com.syedahmed.sukoon) • Direct Support: SYEDAHMED.SA43@GMAIL.COM

Need assistance with SUKOON? Below are answers to the most frequently asked questions regarding offline functionality, prayer times, reminders, and feature settings.

Q. Does SUKOON work without an internet connection?
Yes, 100%. All 114 Surahs, 6,236 Ayahs, the complete Saheeh International English translation, classical Ibn Kathir tafsir summaries, Quranic learning quizzes, and the prayer times calculation engine are pre-bundled directly within the application. You can use SUKOON anywhere—on flights, in remote areas, or without mobile data.
Q. How are prayer times calculated?
SUKOON uses astronomical solar algorithms directly on your phone's processor. It takes your geographic latitude, longitude, and selected calculation convention (such as Muslim World League, ISNA, Umm al-Qura, Egyptian Authority, or Karachi) to compute exact solar transit times. No internet connection is needed for prayer times.
Q. Why do my prayer times differ slightly from my local mosque?
Small differences (typically 2–5 minutes) usually happen because:
  • Calculation Method: Your local mosque may follow a specific convention (e.g., University of Islamic Sciences Karachi or Umm al-Qura). You can adjust this in SUKOON Settings → Prayer Calculation Method.
  • Asr Juristic Method: The standard (Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali) calculates Asr when shadow equals object length; the Hanafi method calculates Asr when shadow equals twice object length. You can toggle this in Settings.
  • Mosque Iqamah Times: Mosques often fix adhan or iqamah times to round minutes (e.g., 5:15 instead of 5:12).
Q. Why am I not receiving prayer or reflection notifications?
If notifications do not appear on time, please check the following Android settings:
  • Allow Notifications: In your phone's Settings → Apps → SUKOON → Notifications, make sure notifications are enabled.
  • Battery Optimization: Aggressive manufacturer battery savers (especially on Xiaomi/MIUI, Samsung, Huawei, OnePlus) can kill background alarms. Go to Settings → Apps → SUKOON → Battery → select “Unrestricted” or “No restrictions”.
  • Alarms & Reminders Permission: Ensure the “Schedule exact alarms” permission is granted in Android system settings.
Q. Where are my bookmarks, streaks, and reflections saved?
Everything is saved locally on your phone in a private SQLite database managed by Android Room. We do not maintain any cloud databases. If you clear app storage or uninstall SUKOON, this data will be cleared.
Q. Are there any ads or hidden paid features?
No, never. SUKOON has zero advertisements and zero paid features. It is created purely as a free, respectful, and accessible Quran companion for everyone around the world.
Q. What Arabic script does SUKOON use?
SUKOON uses the standard Uthmanic Hafs script sourced from the globally verified Tanzil Project, rendered using the classical Amiri Quran typography designed by Khaled Hosny.
