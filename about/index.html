About SUKOON

Vision: Free. Offline. For everyone. • Creator: Syed Ahmed • App ID: com.syedahmed.sukoon
“The Arabic word Sukoon (سُكُون) signifies tranquility, calmness, and peaceful stillness. Our mission is to restore that sacred stillness to your daily Quran reading.”

Our Mission

In an era where digital tools are often cluttered with distracting advertisements, invasive tracking, and forced account registrations, SUKOON was created to offer an alternative: an ad-free, completely private, offline-first Quran companion crafted with reverence.

Whether you are opening the Quran for daily recitation, seeking peace during a difficult moment, studying classical vocabulary, or calculating prayer times on a remote journey, SUKOON is always ready—instantly, privately, and completely offline.

Core Principles

◆ Always Free

No subscriptions, no hidden paywalls, and no monetized Quran content. The Divine Revelation belongs freely to all humanity.

◆ Completely Offline

All 6,236 verses, translations, tafsir summaries, and prayer calculation formulas are stored directly on your device.

◆ Zero Advertisements

No banners, popups, video ads, or sponsored ayahs. Your relationship with the Book of Allah is kept sacred.

◆ Uncompromising Privacy

No login required, no email collected, no tracking cookies, and no cloud surveillance. Everything stays on your phone.

Comprehensive Feature Suite

SUKOON brings together four harmonious pillars of spiritual companion utility:

  • The Quran Reader: Complete 114 Surahs and 30 Juz with authentic Tanzil Uthmanic Arabic, Saheeh International English translation, customizable font sizes, and instant verse search.
  • Prayer Times & Qibla: On-device solar calculations with support for major worldwide calculation methods, punctual local notifications, and an offline Qibla compass.
  • Learning & Quizzes: Scholarly quizzes on surah themes, ayah identification, and verified Arabic vocabulary to deepen your understanding of the Quran.
  • Personal Journey: Private reflection notes, gentle reading streaks, and milestone achievements stored entirely within your local sandbox.
