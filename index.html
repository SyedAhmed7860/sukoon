Welcome to SUKOON

SUKOON is a completely free, peaceful, offline-first Quran companion designed to make the Noble Quran effortless to access, read, reflect upon, and return to. Built with reverence and uncompromising content integrity.

This website serves as the official legal, compliance, and documentation portal for the SUKOON Android application.

✓

100% Free Forever

No subscriptions, no paywalls, and no premium tiers. The complete Quran is freely available to everyone.

✓

Zero Advertisements

No banner ads, no video popups, and no advertising SDKs. A pure, undisturbed spiritual environment.

✓

Total Privacy

No account required, no login, and no data collection. All bookmarks, progress, and reflections stay on your device.
