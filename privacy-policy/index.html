Privacy Policy

Application: SUKOON (com.syedahmed.sukoon) • Effective Date: March 2026 • Developer: Syed Ahmed

Summary: SUKOON is completely free, offline-first, and does NOT collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data with anyone. All your reading history, bookmarks, notes, and preferences remain solely on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile application SUKOON (“the Application”), identified by the package name com.syedahmed.sukoon, developed and maintained by Syed Ahmed (“Developer”, “we”, “us”).

SUKOON was created with a fundamental dedication to user privacy, spiritual serenity, and content integrity. The Noble Quran should be read and reflected upon without surveillance, advertisements, or data harvesting. This policy explains what information is used by the app, how it operates on your device, and confirms that no personal data leaves your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in absolute data minimization. SUKOON does NOT collect or process any of the following:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or government identification.
  • No Account Creation: You do not need to register, create a username, or link social accounts (Google, Facebook, Apple) to use the application.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect Android Advertising IDs (AAID), IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, or hardware fingerprints.
  • No Behavioral Analytics: We do not track which surahs or ayahs you read, how long you spend in the app, or what you search for.
  • No Financial Information: The app is 100% free with no in-app purchases, credit card processing, or financial transactions.
  • No Cloud Sync: No user data is uploaded to remote cloud databases or centralized servers.

3. Device Permissions & On-Device Processing

SUKOON requests a minimal set of Android system permissions strictly required to provide core utility features. All processing happens entirely on your physical device:

A. Location Permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

  • Purpose: Used exclusively to determine your local latitude and longitude to calculate accurate solar Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and the Qibla direction.
  • On-Device Calculation: The prayer times engine performs mathematical astronomical calculations directly on your phone's CPU.
  • Zero Transmission: Your geographic coordinates are never transmitted to any external server, developer, or third-party service.
  • Optional Manual Mode: If you prefer not to grant location permissions, you may select your city or calculation method manually within the app.

B. Notifications Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

  • Purpose: Required on Android 13 (API level 33) and above to display local reminders for prayer times and your optional daily Quran ayah reflections.
  • On-Device Scheduling: Reminders are scheduled locally using Android's native AlarmManager. No external push notification servers (such as Firebase Cloud Messaging) are utilized.
  • User Control: You can enable, disable, or customize notification timings at any time in Settings.

C. Exact Alarm Permission (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)

  • Purpose: Allows the application to schedule precise, punctual notifications at the exact moment a prayer time enters.
  • On-Device Processing: Alarms are configured strictly within the local Android OS alarm framework.

4. On-Device Storage and Data Retention

All information generated while using SUKOON is stored exclusively within your device's private sandbox storage using Android Jetpack Room (SQLite) and Jetpack DataStore:

  • Bookmarks & Favorites: Verses you bookmark are saved locally in your device's database.
  • Reflections & Notes: Private thoughts or reflections you write are stored exclusively in your local database. They are unencrypted within the private app sandbox and are never synchronized to any cloud service.
  • Reading Progress & Streaks: Your last-read position, surah completion milestones, and reading streaks are recorded locally.
  • App Preferences: Font sizes, script styles, theme choices (Light, Dark, OLED), and prayer calculation method preferences are saved locally.

Data Deletion: You retain complete control over your data. You may delete all local data at any time by clearing the application storage in Android Settings (“Clear Data” / “Clear Storage”) or by uninstalling the application.

5. Third-Party Services, SDKs, and Advertisements

SUKOON contains NO advertising networks, NO tracking SDKs, and NO third-party monetization tools. Specifically:

  • We do not integrate Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or any other advertising framework.
  • We do not integrate Google Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, Flurry, or third-party behavioral analytics tools.
  • We do not utilize tracking cookies, web beacons, or tracking pixels.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

SUKOON is designed for users of all ages, including children. Because the application does not collect, retain, or transmit any personal information from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play’s Families Policy. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable European jurisdictions).

7. Security of Your Data

Because we do not transmit or store your personal data on remote servers, your information is protected from remote server data breaches, leaks, and unauthorized cloud access. The security of data stored locally on your device relies on Android's application sandboxing architecture and your device's own lock screen and security settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect updates in Android platform requirements or app enhancements. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated “Effective Date”. Since SUKOON does not collect user contact information, we encourage users to review this page periodically.
